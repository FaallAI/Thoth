# Studio shell v2: a responsive header, phone panes, human labels and truthful beats

Status: draft for operator approval (2026-11-01).
Design source: `design/thoth-dashboard-v2.pen`, the frames named `Studio shell v2 · …` and `Shell v2 · …`:
- the Components and README frames;
- Story at 1440, 1024 (also with the overflow menu open, and in Indonesian) and at 430 (Preview, Materials, Inspector, overflow sheet);
- Timeline at 1440, 1024 (also with the issues list open) and at 430 (Timeline, Assets, issues sheet).

PNG exports are in `D:/thoth-tmp/pen-review/`. The frames were generated by pen.dev from the brief in the 2026-11-01 Studio audit, then corrected on review (see "Design rulings").

## Why

On 2026-11-01 the operator asked for an audit of Studio ("audit page timeline edit sangat berantakan dan studio secara menyeluruh"). The audit found:
- the header wraps at 1440 in Story and stacks its jobs at 1024;
- at 430 wide the page is 985 px;
- assets and clips are named by raw ID (`asset_e9495ab75c…`, `clip_caption_narration_main`);
- the Story inspector shows the scene heading as a 7-line block;
- the beat strip's total disagrees with its beats;
- a completed narration leaves a dead-looking "Create narration" button;
- the Timeline's issues are a count with no way to reach the clip.

The layout regression and the narrated card timing were fixed separately (PR #102). This spec covers the rest.

Success: an operator can edit a draft at 1440 and 1024 without anything wrapping or clipping, and can check and lightly fix a draft on a phone. Every asset and clip is recognisable by name, and the beat strip and issues tell the truth about the draft.

## Goals

1. **Header: 56 px, one row, from 1440 down to 1024.** In both Story and Timeline, and in English and Indonesian.
   - **Tier 1, always shown:** back, draft title, jobs (Edit / Review / Render), mode (Story / Timeline), undo and redo, save status, Request review, overflow button.
   - **Tier 2, Story at 1440 or wider only:** the narration status chip.
   - **Tier 3, the overflow menu:** the narration block (status, Re-voice changed beats, Recreate narration…), then Script, Assist (opens its panel) and Settings.
   - **At 1024:** the back label becomes an icon, the chip folds into the overflow menu, and an amber dot on the overflow button means a beat needs re-voice.
   - **Title:** clamped to one line, with a tooltip holding the full title.
2. **Narration status replaces the dead button.**
   - The states are none, running, awaiting approval, "Narrated · N beats" and failed.
   - "Create narration" exists only while there is no narration.
   - When narrated, the chip opens the narration block. That block offers Re-voice (one beat or every stale beat) and Recreate narration…, which keeps today's confirmation.
3. **Phone (under 768 px): Story and Timeline as panes, with no horizontal page scroll.**
   - **Header:** back, title with a status line (mode · save state), and one overflow button. The button opens a bottom sheet holding the mode, jobs, save state and history, the narration block, Script, Assist, Settings and Request review.
   - **Pane switcher:** Materials / Preview / Inspector in Story; Assets / Preview / Timeline / Inspector in Timeline.
   - **Beats:** a one-row chip scroller with edge fades. The total stays pinned on the left.
   - **Timeline:** view-only lanes with a "Best on a larger screen" note. Tapping a clip shows a summary card with Inspect, and the clip's fields can be edited in the Inspector pane.
4. **Human labels everywhere.** An asset or clip label is "kind · source": for example "Narration · Main", "Comment card · @lailynisfa", "Footage · @OfficialiNews", "Music · …", "Sound effect · whoosh".
   - **Where they show:** asset rows (Story Materials, Timeline Assets, SFX rows), the Story inspector's Shown list, the Timeline inspector title, and timeline clip labels.
   - **What a row shows:** a thumbnail or waveform, the label, a kind icon and the length in seconds. Labels wrap instead of truncating.
   - **No IDs:** an ID never appears.
5. **The Story inspector shows the scene heading as a two-line clamp with Edit.** Edit opens the heading and body fields that exist today, and a tooltip holds the full heading.
6. **The beat strip tells the truth.**
   - Each tile shows its beat's own seconds, and the total equals the canvas length ("matches canvas").
   - A green mic marks a narrated beat. An amber mic marks a stale beat (text changed after recording, from `NarrationState.stale_beats`).
   - Only the selected tile has an outline.
7. **Timeline issues.**
   - The bottom line keeps "N warnings · Review issues".
   - Review issues opens a list grouped by lane: a popover on desktop, a bottom sheet on phone.
   - Each row gives the clip's label, the issue sentence and its time. Clicking a row selects the clip and moves the playhead to it.
   - The selected clip's issues also show in its inspector.

## Non-goals

- **No new warning kinds.** The design shows "gap before a beat", "runs past the end of its beat" and "caption too short". This work lists only the warnings `timelineIssues` produces today; new kinds are a later item.
- No change to Render, Review, the preview player's controls, or how timeline lanes draw clips.
- No server-side asset names, and no migration. Labels come from what the client already has (see "Labels").
- No new editing power on a phone: a phone checks and lightly fixes a draft; it doesn't edit the timeline.

## Anti-goals

- **No lost tools.** The Timeline must keep every tool it has today: trim start and end to the playhead, split, ripple delete, Ripple, Snap and the zoom slider.
- **No lost fields.** The clip inspector must keep Start/End, the Transform presets, Fit, Crop, Offset, Hidden, Locked and Ownership.
- **No uneven card timing.** Nothing may show "each card N s", because the narration shares a beat's time by each card's length (PR #102).
- **No horizontal scroll** at any width from 375 px up.

## Constraints

- **Font:** Geist is the dashboard's font (`--font-sans`). The design frames use `$font-ui` (Geist).
- **Breakpoints:** `studio_viewport.ts` keeps its breakpoints: phone under 768, tablet under 1024, compact desktop under 1440. Today tablet and phone fall back to Quick (`effectiveMode = "simple"`).
- **Copy:** every new string goes in both locales (en, id); the Indonesian 1024 frame is the width test.
- **Responsive tests:** the existing `GuidedStudio.responsive.test.tsx` suite covers the viewports. New layout rules get a test there.
- **Graceful degradation:** without a narration client the narration block is absent, and without materials, labels fall back to the kind ("Image", "Video", "Audio") plus the beat or lane.
- **Rollout:** existing behaviour stays the default until a task replaces it. There is no feature flag. Each task leaves Studio working.

## Decided

- **Phone and tablet get Story or Timeline in panes (v2 drafts).** These widths no longer fall back to Quick. A version 1 draft keeps the scene board, because it has no timeline. The operator approved this on 2026-11-01 ("1. setuju").
- **Design files are tracked.** `design/thoth-dashboard-v2.pen` and its read-only predecessor `design/thoth-dashboard.pen` are committed. Generated exports (`design/exports/`, `design/generated.png`) are ignored. The operator approved this on 2026-11-01.
- **Labels are built client-side.** The sources are the Studio materials (role, owner handle, label, keyed by `asset_id`), the document's clips (`source_role`, the `clip_narration_<beat>` and `clip_sfx_*` prefixes, and the track kind) and the SFX library. An asset none of these name shows "Upload · <kind>".
- **The issues list reuses `timelineIssues` and `issueTarget`.** It groups rows by `target.track_id` and selects through the existing editor actions.
- **Re-voice changed beats is a client loop.** It calls the existing per-beat re-voice for each stale beat, one at a time. There is no new endpoint.

## Design rulings (corrections to the pen.dev frames, already applied in the .pen file)

1. The UI font is Geist, not Inter.
2. Only the selected beat tile has an outline. A stale beat shows an amber mic only.
3. The Timeline toolbar keeps trim, split and ripple delete, plus Ripple, Snap and the zoom slider. At 1024, Ripple and Snap are icon toggles and the slider is shorter.
4. The Timeline inspector keeps the product's fields (Start/End with time and frame, Transform, Clip). Issues sit at the end.
5. Assist is a menu action that opens its panel, not a toggle.
6. The Story inspector's timing reads "Cards follow the voice · N s", not "Each card".
